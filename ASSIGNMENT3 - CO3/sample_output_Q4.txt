=== Q4 EAT, maximum fault rate, frame allocation ===
== Effective access time: EAT = (1-p)*100 + p*8,000,000 ns ==
p = 1 in 1,000      EAT =    8099.90 ns   slow-down =    7999.9%  (81.0x)
p = 1 in 100,000    EAT =     180.00 ns   slow-down =      80.0%  (1.8x)
p = 1 in 1,000,000  EAT =     108.00 ns   slow-down =       8.0%  (1.1x)

Max page-fault rate for a 10% slow-down (EAT <= 110 ns): p <= 10 / (8,000,000 - 100) = 1.250e-06 = 1 fault per 799990 accesses

== Frame allocation: 62 free frames, processes of 10 and 127 pages ==
equal allocation        : 31 and 31 frames
proportional allocation : 10 x 62 / 137 = 4.53 -> 4 and 127 x 62 / 137 = 57.47 -> 57 frames

== Alternative: 32 GB RAM, no paging to disk (rate measured by Q4_touch is used in the README) ==
EAT with no faults = 100 ns; with p=1e-5: 180.0 ns; cost of extra RAM must be compared with the run-time saving.
