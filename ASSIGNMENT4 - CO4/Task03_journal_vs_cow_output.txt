Extra space (CoW): checksums 4 B per 4 KiB block = 0.098%; each snapshot keeps only
blocks changed since it was taken (O(changed blocks)), old blocks are freed on delete.
Recovery after power failure: ext4 replays the journal, time O(journal size, e.g. 128 MiB-1 GiB);
CoW mounts the last committed root, O(1) (plus an optional intent-log replay in ZFS).
